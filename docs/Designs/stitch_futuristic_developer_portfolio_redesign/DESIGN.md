## Brand & Style

This design system channels an aggressive, ultra-modern cyber-developer aesthetic inspired by high-performance engineering portfolios and developer terminals. It pairs deep pitch-black obsidian foundations with high-voltage neon acid lime highlights, projecting precision, cutting-edge technical fluency, and electric visual momentum.

### Target Audience & Voice
- **Audience:** Tech founders, engineering directors, VC scouts, and design engineers seeking forward-looking software craftsmanship.
- **Personality:** Authoritative, uncompromisingly technical, futuristic, energetic, and razor-sharp.
- **Design Movement:** Neo-Brutalist Cyberpunk infused with minimal dark UI. Structural layout discipline meets intense fluorescent energy through luminous borders, high-contrast monospace micro-labels, and neon directional glyphs.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around maximum optical tension between total light absorption and radioactive luminescence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D0FD1B` / `#CCFF00`):** The signature hyper-acid electric lime. Reserved strictly for critical calls-to-action, section kickers, glowing state markers, and active interactive borders.
- **Secondary (`#16181D`):** Deep charcoal slate for container surfaces, interactive cards, and tile backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#22252A`):** Low-light structural strokes and crisp dividers that segment the UI without breaking immersion.
- **Neutral (`#080808`):** Pure obsidian baseline that anchors the entire visual viewport.

### Text Contrast Roles
- **Primary Text (`#FFFFFF`):** High-impact headline white, ensuring effortless readability against dark backdrops.
- **Secondary Text (`#A1A1AA`):** Cool silver-grey for paragraph summaries, project descriptions, and sub-headings.
- **Muted Text (`#52525B`):** Compact timestamps, stack footnotes, and decorative terminal metadata.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances dramatic editorial scale with technical precision:

- **Display & Main Headings (`Bebas Neue`):** All-caps display style delivering massive vertical gravity, bold impact, and industrial energy.
- **Section Kickers & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Monospace-leaning geometric sans applied in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.15em`). Preceded by neon directional indicators (`▶`) to reinforce terminal navigation.
- **Body & Interactive Content (`Outfit`):** Geometric, crystal-clear readability that retains modern circular geometry while remaining legible at small paragraph sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid system bounded by a maximum width of `1280px` (`max-w-7xl`), centered within the viewport.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Flow
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column container stack. Section margin shifts to `1.25rem`, element gutters collapse to `1rem`. Hero portrait and headline stack vertically.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 6-column grid; project and credential cards reflow into 2-column matrices.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** Full 12-column grid. Grid cards for projects and certifications scale into 3-column rows (`col-span-4`), skills expand across a high-density 6-column icon grid.

### Vertical Rhythm
Major content sections are separated by expansive breathing room (`space-3xl`, ~96px) to let self-contained modules command attention without visual noise.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** coupled with **Luminescent Edge Glows**:

- **Baseline Level (0):** `#080808` canvas surface with no borders.
- **Surface Level (1):** `#121316` card container with a crisp `1px` border in `#22252A`.
- **Active / Interactive Hover (2):** Surface tint shifts to `#16181D`, the border sharpens to `#D0FD1B`, and a diffuse outer halo emerges: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(208, 253, 27, 0.18)`.
- **Top Bar Floating Navigation:** Fixed top navbar uses backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur-md`) with `rgba(8, 8, 8, 0.85)` surface opacity and a micro-border `rgba(34, 37, 42, 0.8)` along its bottom edge.

## Shapes

The design system maintains controlled geometric structure using balanced radii (`0.5rem` / `8px` default):

- **Default UI Elements & Tiles (`rounded-md`):** Buttons, skill cards, and project containers feature 8px corners to preserve a sharp, modern developer feel without feeling harsh.
- **Badges & Action Pills (`rounded-full`):** Status badges, technology tags, and primary action CTAs employ pill geometry (`9999px`) to contrast against rectangular card boundaries.
- **Image Containers:** Project preview frames use a 12px outer radius with inner border clipping to highlight application screenshots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid neon fill (`#D0FD1B`), pitch-black text (`#000000`), uppercase `Space Grotesk` font with `font-weight: 700`. Rounded pill shape. On hover, background lifts to `#CCFF00` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(208, 253, 27, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary / Outline Action:** Dark surface `#121316` with a `1px` solid border (`#2A2E35`), neon lime text (`#D0FD1B`), and pill or rounded-md form. On hover, border transitions to `#D0FD1B` with subtle scale shift (`scale(1.02)`).

### Section Kickers
- Composed of a neon green right-triangle (`▶`), followed by letter-spaced uppercase text in `#D0FD1B` (`0.15em` letter-spacing). Underneath sits a massive headline in `Bebas Neue` followed by an optional accent rule (`2px` height, `#D0FD1B`, width `48px`).

### Project Showcase Cards
- Dark obsidian base (`#121316`) enclosed in a razor-thin border (`#22252A`).
- **Header Asset:** High-resolution screenshot framed with subtle inner border overlay.
- **Tech Stack Strip:** Row of monospace micro-pills with dark slate backgrounds and neon lime text (e.g., `NEXT • TYPESCRIPT • POSTGRES`).
- **Body & Footer:** Bold project title in `#FFFFFF`, concise silver description (`#A1A1AA`), and a text link (`VIEW PROJECT ➔`) that transforms to full neon glow on hover.

### Tech Skill Grid Tiles
- Square or rounded-md tiles (`80px` to `100px` minimum bounding box) built with `#121316`.
- Centered brand/tool monochrome or high-chroma logo with uppercase label (`11px`, `#A1A1AA`) placed below.
- Hover state: Border shifts to `#D0FD1B` with a `4px` upward translation (`translate-y-[-4px]`).

### Experience & Education Timeline
- Vertical dashed or solid `2px` guide stroke in `#22252A`.
- Milestone nodes: Outer circle (`12px`) filled with `#080808` and ringed by `#D0FD1B` with an inner solid neon core (`6px`).
- Connected card: Displays year span tag (`Space Grotesk`, `#D0FD1B`), role title (`Outfit Bold`, `#FFFFFF`), institution/company, and bulleted deliverables in `#A1A1AA`.

### Lightbulb Theme Toggle
- Minimalist dangling bulb icon centered at the top of the hero section.
- Amber-to-lime glow filament when illuminated, crisp outline in dark mode with a faint ray beam effect.
